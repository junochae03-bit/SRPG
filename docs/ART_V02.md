## 최종 생성 프롬프트

Create ONE production game UI sprite atlas, 2048x2048 square, exact 4 columns by 4 rows of equal square cells. Truly transparent alpha background, no visible grid, no labels/text or outer frames. Each separate icon centered in its own 512x512 cell, 72% cell occupancy with clear transparent gutters. Hand-painted approachable Korean fantasy RPG inventory illustrations, clean warm dark-brown outlines, chunky readable silhouettes, softly shaded steel/leather, restrained gold/teal accents, warm daylight, NOT realistic, NOT neon, no gore. All objects at matching perceived scale, readable at 48px. Row 1 from left: (1) short guardian sword with small shield (2) elegant duelist longsword (3) blue rune engraved blade (4) summoner's open magic grimoire with small green gem. Row 2: (5) elemental staff with blue red crystal head (6) healer holy focus with sun-shaped gold charm (7) long precision sniper bow (8) rugged hunter bow with feather. Row 3: (9) compact explorer recurve bow and compass charm (10) pair of curved thief daggers (11) short reaper sickle with looping chain (12) fan of four playing cards with red black suit motifs. Row 4: (13) paired steel infighter gauntlets (14) single heavy breaker gauntlet with thick knuckle plates (15) pair of martial artist cloth hand wraps with brass wrist cuffs (16) friendly leather-gloved open hand reaching to pick up an object, interaction icon. Maintain consistent illustrated fantasy style throughout the atlas. No UI backgrounds, no panels, no checkerboard drawn into image.
