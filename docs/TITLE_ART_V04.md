# V0.4 title illustration

Selected runtime asset: `game/assets/ui/title-storybook-v04.png` (1586×992, opaque PNG).

Created with the built-in image generation tool on 2026-09-09. The user requested the feeling of an early Korean 2D RPG title screen: a woodland illustration in an open book and wooden menu boards. The logo and every menu label/button are rendered by Godot using the project's DNF fonts. They are not baked into the illustration. The first grand landscape concept was superseded and is not included in the game or repository.

Final prompt:

> Use case: stylized-concept. Production asset: the whole background art for a charming old-fashioned 2D Korean RPG title screen. Wide landscape 16:10 aspect. Create a SIMPLE COZY HAND-PAINTED STORYBOOK SCENE, with the modest, friendly look of a 2003-era Korean sprite MMO menu. This is a little illustrated woodland page in an open book, NOT epic concept art. Thick cream paper border all around, softly curved layered page edges and book binding visible at the bottom left, warm brown cloth book cover peeking out, the forest picture sits within the page. Inside the illustration: one big rounded mossy tree trunk taking up the left third, a few orange and yellow mushrooms at its base, chunky grass tufts, rounded emerald and jade forest foliage behind, a simple sunny path going into soft turquoise woodland light. Flat appealing forms with hand-painted shading, smooth cartoon silhouettes, a limited warm saturated but natural palette, confident 2D game art. On the RIGHT lower half, an empty rustic wooden signboard standing on two short posts, made of four wide brown wooden horizontal planks with simple carved bevels, completely BLANK front with NO text. The empty signboard occupies approximately x=55% to88%, y=48% to80%, and faces the viewer square-on so actual game menu buttons can be overlaid. Leave a quiet open turquoise forest-light area at x=29% to78%,y=15% to43% for a game logo later. All content within safe paper border. Gentle dappled sunlight. No characters, no logos, no letters, no numbers, no maple-leaf trademark, no UI labels, no watermark. No castle, no grand gate, no cliffs, no epic vista, no photorealistic textures, no intricate ornament, no gold baroque frames, no excessive detail, no 3D rendering. The mood is humble, nostalgic, playful and welcoming: a tiny adventure in a storybook.

Integration: `game/scripts/title_screen.gd`. The same background remains behind the character creation sheet. No gameplay environment packs from the other art tasks are changed by this asset.
